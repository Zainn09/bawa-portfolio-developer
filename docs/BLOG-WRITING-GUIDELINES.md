STRICT BLOG WRITING AND HUMANIZATION GUIDELINES

The last batch of blogs was pathetic. The writing was overly polished, repetitive, formulaic, and noticeably AI-like. Fix the problems in the **last batch only**. Do not rewrite older batches, unrelated articles, or other content.

Follow these instructions strictly for all revisions in the current batch.

1. Write Like a Real Human Expert

- Use natural, professional language that sounds like an experienced person explaining something they genuinely understand.
- Avoid generic AI-style phrasing, predictable transitions, and overly polished, motivational conclusions.
- Vary sentence length, paragraph structure, and rhythm naturally.
- Do not force every paragraph to sound clever or end with a memorable statement.
- Use first-person language only when it adds genuine perspective or explains a real professional judgment.
- Avoid unnecessary jargon, inflated language, and repetitive explanations.

2. Eliminate Common AI-Writing Patterns

- Remove repetitive sentence structures and predictable rhetorical contrasts.
- Avoid repeatedly using constructions such as “This is not just X; it is Y,” “That distinction matters,” “Here is the honest boundary,” and similar formulaic expressions.
- Do not repeatedly restate the same central argument using different words.
- Avoid excessive summary sentences, artificial emphasis, and unnecessary concluding statements.
- Remove generic transitions and headings that add no meaningful information.
- Do not make every section follow the same introduction, explanation, takeaway pattern.

3. Prioritize Specificity Over Fluff

- Replace vague claims with concrete explanations, practical examples, and relevant technical details.
- Include real observations, implementation decisions, limitations, or troubleshooting details whenever verified information is available.
- Clearly distinguish verified facts, illustrative examples, recommendations, and personal opinions.
- Never fabricate client experiences, research findings, measurements, performance improvements, or commercial results.
- Do not invent details merely to make the writing sound more human.

4. Improve Structure and Readability

- Remove redundant paragraphs and unnecessary sections.
- Use headings only when they help readers navigate the material.
- Keep explanations direct and useful.
- Avoid excessive bullet points, FAQs, summaries, and repetitive takeaways.
- Make each section contribute new information rather than repeating earlier sections.
- Preserve technical accuracy while simplifying unnecessarily complicated language.

5. Preserve Authenticity and Credibility

- Retain the author's genuine voice and professional perspective.
- Avoid pretending to have personally performed work or verified results when that has not happened.
- Support factual and platform-specific claims with reliable sources where appropriate.
- Do not make unsupported guarantees about SEO rankings, AI visibility, traffic, conversions, or business outcomes.
- Ensure examples and references are accurate and relevant to the article.

6. Strict Scope: Fix Only the Last Batch

- Review and revise only the most recent batch of blogs.
- Do not modify previous batches, unrelated documents, website content, or other deliverables.
- Preserve each article's intended topic, technical purpose, factual meaning, and relevant internal links.
- Do not introduce unnecessary rewrites where a focused edit will solve the problem.
- Apply these standards consistently across every blog in the latest batch.

7. Final Quality-Control Check

Before submitting each revised blog, verify that:

- The writing sounds natural rather than formulaically polished.
- Repeated arguments and unnecessary sentences have removed.
- The article contains useful, specific information rather than generic filler.
- Sentence structures and paragraph lengths vary naturally.
- Every factual claim is defensible.
- No fabricated evidence, experiences, or results have been introduced.
- The article reads like credible professional writing, not a generic AI-generated SEO template.

**Final instruction:** Do not simply replace AI-sounding phrases with synonyms. Fix the underlying writing problems. Make the content clearer, more specific, less repetitive, and more authentic.
